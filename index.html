Our Story

Three Decades of Crunchy Goodness

Asal Ratlami Sev Bhandar was founded with a simple mission — to bring the authentic, fiery taste of Ratlam's famous sev to the streets of Ahmedabad.

Located on the bustling Naroda Road, our shop has become a beloved destination for namkeen lovers across the city. Every batch is prepared fresh daily using traditional recipes passed down through generations.
